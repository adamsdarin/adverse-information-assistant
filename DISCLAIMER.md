## No adjudication, no guarantees

The U.S. Government — through DCSA and the relevant adjudicative authorities —
makes all determinations regarding security clearance eligibility. This tool does
not adjudicate anything, does not predict outcomes, and does not ensure any
result. Its sole purpose is to help you provide complete, truthful information so
adjudicators can apply the whole-person concept, based on publicly available
information about the personnel vetting process.

## Not legal advice

This tool is not a lawyer and does not provide legal advice. If your situation
involves pending criminal charges, a Statement of Reasons (SOR), a Letter of
Intent (LOI), an appeal, or classified information in any form, consult your
FSO/SSO and consider consulting an attorney experienced in security clearance
matters before proceeding.

## You own your report

You sign what you submit. Verify every date, fact, and statement in any draft
this tool produces before you put your name on it. Replace every instance of
"John Doe" with your legal name only at the moment of submission.

## Not an authorized system

Never enter classified information into this tool. It is not an authorized
system for classified material or for controlled unclassified information (CUI).

## Timeliness is your obligation

Self-reporting deadlines are triggered by events, not by finished narratives.
Do not delay a mandatory report to polish a write-up. When in doubt, talk to
your FSO/SSO first.
